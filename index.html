FAQ

Questions, answered straight

What is included in a standard clean?

A standard clean covers the whole home, bedrooms and bathrooms included. Your written quote locks in the price for your home before tax.

Do cleaners bring their own supplies?

Yes. Cleaners bring their own supplies.

What about pets?

Tell us about your pets when you call or email to book, so your cleaner can plan around them. Contact us with any questions.

What happens if no one is home, or you cannot get in?

Contact us before your clean to arrange access. If we arrive and cannot get in, contact us and we will work it out with you. We do not publish a lockout or show-up fee; that policy is still being decided.

What is your guarantee?

If we miss a checklist item, report it within 48 hours with a photo and we will fix it with a touch-up from the original cleaner at no extra pay. The guarantee covers missed checklist items only; it is not a full re-clean.

How do I cancel or reschedule?

Contact us as soon as you can and we will cancel or reschedule your clean. We do not publish a cancellation fee.
