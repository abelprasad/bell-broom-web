Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This page is a draft for review before go-live. It is not legal advice.

What information we collect

When you call or email us, we collect only the information you share with us to arrange a cleaning, such as:

  • Your name
  • Your phone number
  • Your email address
  • Your home address
  • Home details needed for a quote or a clean, such as the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, the type of service you want, and any notes about your home

We do not ask for payment card details, government ID numbers, or any other sensitive information by phone or email.

How we use your information

We use the information you share only to:

  • Respond to your call or email
  • Prepare a quote
  • Book and carry out your cleaning

We do not use your information for any other purpose.

Sharing your information

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your personal information with third parties for marketing. Your details are used only within Bell & Broom Cleaning LLC to arrange and carry out your cleaning.

Cookies and analytics

This website does not run analytics and does not use cookies. We do not track visitors to this site.

Questions about this policy

If you have a question about this policy or about the information you shared with us, contact us at hello@bellandbroom.com or call (609) 526-8336.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the “Last updated” date at the top of this page.
